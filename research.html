Research & Thinking

The questions
behind my work.

I am interested in questions that sit between academic research, institutional practice and everyday life. My research interests explore how culture, identity, institutional assumptions and structural inequalities influence the way people experience public systems.

Research Themes

01

Race, Gender & Institutional Recognition

How do cultural assumptions influence the way institutions perceive and respond to Black women and girls?

02

Adultification & Safeguarding

How do institutional perceptions of childhood, vulnerability and responsibility influence safeguarding outcomes?

03

Social Policy & Institutional Accountability

How can public policy better reflect the realities of the communities it is designed to serve?

04

Medical Anthropology & Health Inequality

How do culture, structural inequalities and institutional practices shape access to healthcare and experiences of wellbeing?

05

Qualitative Research & Lived Experience

How can qualitative research complement quantitative data to create a more comprehensive understanding of social challenges?

06

Technology, Data & Social Inequality

How can emerging technologies and data-driven systems account for cultural context, representation and the experiences of marginalised communities?

Featured Publication

First-Class Dissertation

Visibly Invisible

Exploring the Impact of Adultification and Age Misrecognition on Black Women and Girls

This First-Class undergraduate dissertation in Anthropology examines how cultural assumptions and institutional perceptions shape the recognition and treatment of Black women and girls, interrogating who institutions recognise as vulnerable, and whose experiences are reflected in decision-making.
