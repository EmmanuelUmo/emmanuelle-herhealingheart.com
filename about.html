Full Biography

Emmanuelle Faith Zoe is an anthropologist, emerging social policy researcher, strategic communications professional and social impact founder based in London.

A First-Class BSc Anthropology graduate, her academic and professional interests centre on social policy, race and gender equality, institutional accountability and social innovation.

Her anthropological perspective informs how she approaches complex social challenges. She is particularly interested in the gaps between policy intention and lived experience: who institutions recognise as vulnerable, whose experiences are reflected in the data, whose voices inform decision-making, and who ultimately benefits from the systems we build.

Her undergraduate dissertation, Visibly Invisible: Exploring the Impact of Adultification and Age Misrecognition on Black Women and Girls, examined how cultural assumptions and institutional perceptions shape the recognition and treatment of Black women and girls.

Beyond academia, Emmanuelle works across strategic communications, campaigns and social impact entrepreneurship. She is the founder of Her Healing Heart, a community-based wellness and social impact venture, and FAITHZOE Group, her wider entrepreneurial platform.

Her long-term ambition is to contribute to more culturally informed approaches to research, institutional change and social innovation, including the development of a culturally informed research and policy platform, bringing together academic research, institutional insight, community knowledge and storytelling to help shape better social outcomes.
