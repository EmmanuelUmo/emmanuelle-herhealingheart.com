Introduction

Understanding people. Questioning systems. Imagining better futures.

I am an anthropologist, emerging social policy researcher, strategic communications professional and social impact founder exploring how people experience institutions, how systems shape opportunity, and how we can build solutions that better reflect the realities of the communities they serve.

My work brings together cultural research, social policy, storytelling and community-led innovation to examine the relationship between individual experiences and the wider structures that shape them.

With a First-Class BSc in Anthropology, I am particularly interested in who institutions recognise as vulnerable, whose experiences are reflected in the data, whose voices inform decision-making, and who ultimately benefits from the systems we build.
